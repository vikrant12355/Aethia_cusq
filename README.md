# Aethia Trade

**Institutional AI Consensus Gateways & Blockchain Auditing**

Aethia Trade is a next-generation wealth management platform that leverages a multi-agent AI consensus engine (incorporating models like GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, and Gemini 1.5) to determine optimal institutional portfolio strategies based on deep behavioral client profiling. 

All execution strategies are cryptographically anchored to the Polygon blockchain for immutable audit trails and routed seamlessly via prime brokerage APIs.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Zero-Knowledge Private Onboarding**: Establish client risk tolerance, loss aversion indices, and anomaly scores securely.
* **AI Consensus Engine**: Multiple LLM models debate and reach a mathematical consensus on the optimal asset allocation, optimizing for max drawdown and Sharpe ratio.
* **Explainable AI (SHAP)**: Understand exactly *why* the models chose specific strategies with clear feature attributions and rationales.
* **Cryptographic Execution Audit**: Immutable execution receipts anchored on the Polygon blockchain (Keccak-256 / EIP-712).
* **Institutional Dashboard**: A BlackRock Aladdin-inspired UI providing real-time portfolio positions and audit ledger tracking.
